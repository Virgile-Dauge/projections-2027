# Ecological Inference
Gary King, Ori Rosen, Martin Tanner. 2006. "Ecological Inference". _In The New Palgrave Dictionary of Economics, edited by Larry Blume and Steven N. Durlauf, 2nd ed. London: Palgrave_.
[ Book Chapter ](https://gking.harvard.edu/files/eidef.pdf) Cite
### Abstract
Dictionary entry on the definition of “ecological inference,” and a brief summary of the history of ecological inference research.
