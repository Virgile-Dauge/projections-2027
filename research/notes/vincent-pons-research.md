summary: 'Vincent Pons''s HBS faculty publication list (accessed 2026). Source-hub
  / bibliography page, not itself a citable finding, but the load-bearing index for
  locating his other work on electoral targeting and forecasting relevant to this
  research query: ''Pitfalls of Demographic Forecasts of US Elections'' (NBER WP 33016,
  Oct 2024, with Calvo & Shapiro) directly critiques demographic-based projection
  methods; ''Sources and Extent of Rising Partisan Segregation in the U.S. — Evidence
  from 143 Million Voters'' (NBER WP 33422, Jan 2025) is a precinct/individual-level
  geographic partisan-sorting study; ''Increasing the Electoral Participation of Immigrants:
  Experimental Evidence from France'' (Economic Journal 2019, with Liégey — Liégey
  co-founded the porte-à-porte firm behind Hollande''s 2012 campaign) and ''Voter
  Registration Costs and Disenfranchisement: Experimental Evidence from France'' (APSR
  2017) are French field-experiment papers relevant to abstention modeling. Also lists
  ''Contre l''abstention: campagnes de terrain et simplification des règles électorales''
  (2023 book chapter) and the book ''Porte à porte: Reconquérir la démocratie sur
  le terrain'' (Pons, Liégey & Muller, 2013) — the French-language field manual/theory
  behind the 2012 door-to-door operation.'
