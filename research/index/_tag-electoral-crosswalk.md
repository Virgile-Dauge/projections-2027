(2) Precincts are edited (split, merged, redrawn) in roughly one-sixth of geographic units (counties, or town/city-equivalents in CT/ME/MA/NH/RI/VT and legislative districts in AK/DE) EVERY election cycle for administrative reasons -- direct evidence that precinct/bureau-de-vote boundary instability across cycles (2022 to 2027 in the French case) is the norm, not the exception. (5) Final vote tallies are cross-validated against official county-level totals; discrepancies are investigated and are usually (but not always) VEST data-production errors.
