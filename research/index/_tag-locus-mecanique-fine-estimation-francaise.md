Ipsos selected 1,180 polling stations (bureaux de vote) total, structured in three nested tiers: 450 stations nationally for the 20h national estimate; ~40 stations per each of the 21 continental regions for regional-level estimates; and, in the three flagship regions (Ile-de-France, Nord-Pas-de-Calais, PACA), 30 stations per constituent département for department-level precision, enabling seat projections region-by-region at staggered times (Nord-Pas-de-Calais at 20h, PACA at 21h, Ile-de-France at 21h30). The 1,180 correspondents phoned in results in three waves: mid-afternoon turnout, ~19h count of the first 200 ballots, and ~19h30 complete station results (for stations closing at 18h).
